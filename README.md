## Overview

The home of *Love Is Here* — a release platform built to do one job properly: get a listener from arriving on the page to actually hearing the record, and give an independent artist a permanent address that isn't rented from a streaming service.

### Key features

- **Album listening experience** — the record front and centre, not buried under a press bio
- **Video feature** — the *Working On It* visual embedded as a first-class moment on the page
- **Dark editorial design** — a deep, low-light palette built for a soul and smooth-jazz record rather than a generic artist template
- **Motion and scroll choreography** — animated reveals that carry the eye down the page without getting in the way of the music
- **Full social preview handling** — Open Graph and Twitter cards so every shared link renders as artwork instead of a bare URL
- **Support and direct-connect paths** — routes for listeners to back the work directly

## Built for

**Eric Gray** — musician, producer and smooth jazz guitarist, and Executive Producer of **Poor Righteous Teachers**. A career spanning hip-hop, R&B, soul and jazz, released and promoted independently.

### The brief

An artist with a real catalogue and a new album, but no owned home on the web — everything living on streaming platforms and social feeds he doesn't control. The site needed to:

- Make *Love Is Here* the centre of gravity, with listening one click from landing
- Carry the weight of a long career without turning into a wall of biography
- Look like the record sounds — warm, dark, grown, unhurried
- Give press, collaborators and listeners a single canonical link to point at
- Load fast and preview cleanly wherever the link gets shared

### Delivered by Naga Codex

Concept and art direction, interface design, front-end build, motion design, media and video integration, social preview and metadata setup, and production deployment.
